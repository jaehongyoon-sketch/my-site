# Kalshi evaluation questions

Draft only. No inquiry has been sent and no approval is assumed.

1. Can a commercial third-party app display your public market data to US customers? Which written agreement covers display, redistribution, caching, historical storage, and derived analytics?
2. Is any commercial access tier free? Please specify setup charges, minimum commitments, request or account fees, overages, market-data fees, attribution, and termination requirements.
3. May we support customers trading in their own Kalshi accounts? What registration, approved partner role, or other eligibility is required of the app operator? Can a solo developer apply before incorporation?
4. Which delegated authorization flow is supported? What scopes, user consent, revocation, credential storage, and account recovery requirements apply?
5. Who handles onboarding, KYC, sanctions checks, location and eligibility checks, custody, deposits, withdrawals, settlement, statements, and customer complaints?
6. What sandbox access is available before a commercial agreement? Are test balances and nonproduction orders available for this proposed integration model?
7. What production and sandbox rate limits, WebSocket permissions, caching limits, uptime expectations, and change-notification policies apply?
8. How should we handle fractional-cent pricing, current fee schedules, executable quotes, market halts, partial fills, cancellation races, corrections, and settlement reversals?
9. Which endpoints or signed events support idempotency, replay, account reconciliation, incident investigation, and audit retention?
10. Are referral fees, subscriptions, or other revenue models permitted? Are platform-added transaction fees prohibited or subject to additional approval and disclosures?
11. What branding, marketing, risk disclosures, app-store distribution, and country/state restrictions would apply?
12. What security review, penetration testing, insurance, business documentation, operational coverage, and launch approval are required? Who is the technical and incident contact after onboarding?

Before sending: insert the founder's contact details and accurate entity/jurisdiction information, confirm the intended legal role with suitable advisers, and attach the product brief. Send only after the founder explicitly instructs it.
